Stable and Safe Neural ODEs

Tianyi Xia, Aditya Vats

  • Design a Neural ODE motion policy jointly trained with a learned Warped-ICNN Lyapunov function, enforcing CLF stability and CBF obstacle avoidance through closed-form half-space projections in the forward pass.
  • Trained end-to-end through the projection layer to eliminate train/test mismatch, using tube-based off-demo sampling to shape the raw field. Proposed a two-stage residual variant that leaves the stable base field exactly unchanged outside the obstacle neighborhood.
  • Outperformed LPV-DS on 3 LASA shapes (up to 5.0× lower DTWD and 5.8× lower velocity-direction error), with zero barrier violations over ~46k rollout points in 2D and 3D obstacle-avoidance tests.
