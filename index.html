Tianyi Xia

I'm a master's student in Robotics at the GRASP Lab, University of Pennsylvania, advised by Nadia Figueroa and Nikolai Matni. I work on safe and stable robot learning: learning motion policies from demonstrations and giving them provable stability and safety guarantees with tools from control theory, such as Lyapunov functions, control barrier functions, and reachability analysis.

I received my B.S. in Information and Computing Science and B.Eng. in Cyberspace Security from Harbin Institute of Technology, and was a visiting student at UC Berkeley and UC San Diego. I have also worked on human–robot interaction at the Pervasive HCI Group, Tsinghua University.

Research

My research focuses on developing safe, learning-based control for harmonious human–robot interaction.

Reach-Avoid Certificates for Contact-based Stochastic Hybrid Systems
Tianyi Xia, Ryan K. Cosner
American Control Conference (ACC), 2027 (under review)
paper

Introduced reach-avoid contact certificates that guarantee, over an infinite horizon and with a prescribed probability, that a stochastic hybrid system controlled only through its resets reaches the target before the unsafe set. Certificates are found by linear programming and turned into a pointwise safety filter for arbitrary nominal controllers.

A Reachability-based Safety Certificate for Dynamical System Motion Policies
Aditya Vats*, Tianyi Xia*, Nadia Figueroa
IEEE Transactions on Robot Learning (T-RL), 2026 (under review)
Spotlight, IROS 2026 Workshop on Hybrid Architectures for Embodied Autonomy
paper

Developed a reachability-based safety certificate for DS motion policies that reduces the infinite-horizon problem to a finite-horizon rollout while provably recovering the maximal forward-invariant safe set. Its look-ahead safety filter avoids the spurious attractors and deadlocks of modulation and CBF methods, validated on five DS classes and a Franka manipulator.
