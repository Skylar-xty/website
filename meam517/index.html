Viability-Preserving Passive Torque Control for Mobile Base Manipulator

Aditya Vats, Tianyi Xia, Sumukh Shrote

  • Extended Viability-Preserving Passive Torque Control (VPP-TC) from a fixed-base arm to a 9-DoF mobile manipulator (7-DoF arm on a 2-DoF omnidirectional base), keeping a passive DS-based torque controller inside precomputed viable sets for joint limits, self-collision, and external collision via an instantaneous QP.
  • Learned the self-collision viable set with a Transformer classifier (99.69% accuracy, 99.72% recall on 8M sampled states) and encoded external-collision viability with Bernstein-polynomial link SDFs, benchmarking against a CBF-QP baseline (CPIC) with identical constraints in PyBullet.
  • Ran at 110 Hz vs. 47 Hz for the Hessian-dependent CBF baseline, converging 3× faster (3 s vs. 9 s) with 6–11× lower RMS end-effector jerk while keeping tracking collision-free.
