Autonomous Quadrotor Flight through Cluttered Mazes

Sanghyub (Paul) Lee, Angelina Zhang, Parra Wang, Tianyi Xia

  • Deployed a full planning-and-control stack on a Crazyflie 2.0 quadrotor in a VICON motion-capture arena: A* search on an inflated 3D occupancy grid, greedy shortcutting into sparse waypoints, and minimum-jerk quintic splines continuous up to snap.
  • Implemented a geometric SE(3) tracking controller with a PD position loop and a nonlinear attitude loop, and retuned its gains on hardware to handle actuator dynamics, sensor noise, and model mismatch absent in simulation.
  • Flew all three mazes autonomously with 0.02–0.06 m lateral and about 0.12 m vertical tracking RMSE.
